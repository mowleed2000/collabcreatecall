What actually happens when a customer calls?

Every conversation follows a purposeful commercial path designed around what your business needs to happen next.

1

Inbound Greeting

Greets the caller using the wording and routing rules agreed for your business.

Business details change constantly: pricing updates, seasonal hours, new staff, and shifting service areas. When a company buys a self-serve AI tool, the prompt quickly becomes outdated and callers receive stale information.

With CCM, your voice agent is treated as a managed operational asset. When your services or hours change, we make the approved updates, test the call paths, and review caller transcripts to keep the system performing at its peak.

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers for business owners.

Will callers know they are speaking with an AI assistant?

Yes. Transparency and professionalism are central to our setup. The agent introduces itself clearly and professionally as your business's automated assistant, designed to help immediately without making the caller wait on hold. The goal is speed, accuracy, and professional courtesy.

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Often an existing business number can be kept using call forwarding, depending on your phone provider and current setup. We check the available routing options with you before recommending an approach.

What happens if someone has an urgent emergency or complex question?

We agree in advance what the agent should do when a caller needs a person. Depending on your phone setup, that may mean routing the call or recording the enquiry for a team member to follow up.

Which software tools, CRMs, and calendars can it connect with?

We review your phone system and existing software during discovery. Any connection to calendars, booking tools or CRMs is confirmed after checking compatibility and scope.

How long does implementation take?

Implementation timing depends on the call flow, integrations and information needed. We confirm a realistic schedule after understanding your requirements and before work begins.

What does the ongoing management service include?

We don't leave you with a login and walk away. Our managed service includes reviewing call transcripts, refining conversation prompts, updating business information (hours, prices, staff changes), resolving any logic issues, and monthly performance reviews.
