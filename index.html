Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers for business owners.

Will callers know they are speaking with an AI assistant?

Yes. Transparency and professionalism are central to our setup. The agent introduces itself clearly and professionally as your business's automated assistant, designed to help immediately without making the caller wait on hold. The goal is speed, accuracy, and professional courtesy.

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

Yes, absolutely. You do not need to change your advertised number. You simply set up standard call forwarding (either all calls, overflow when busy, or after-hours only) from your existing line to your dedicated agent number.

What happens if someone has an urgent emergency or complex question?

Every agent is configured with strict escalation and human handoff rules. If a caller requires immediate human intervention, the agent can instantly warm-transfer the call to a designated mobile number, or dispatch an urgent priority SMS and email alert to your on-call team.

Which software tools, CRMs, and calendars can it connect with?

We configure direct integrations with popular calendars (Google Calendar, Outlook/Office 365, Calendly), booking engines, and CRM systems (HubSpot, ServiceM8, Zapier webhooks, and custom endpoints) subject to API compatibility.

How long does implementation take?

Typical setup from discovery consultation to live deployment takes 5 to 10 business days. This allows adequate time to map conversation logic, configure business information, test edge cases, and run representative test calls with your approval.

What does the ongoing management service include?

We don't leave you with a login and walk away. Our managed service includes reviewing call transcripts, refining conversation prompts, updating business information (hours, prices, staff changes), resolving any logic issues, and monthly performance reviews.
