Our 7-Stage Customer Lifecycle

How we take your call handling from friction to flow.

We follow a clear process to agree the call flow, configure the service and review it with your team before launch.

01

Discover

We review the calls you want to handle, opening hours, common questions, service boundaries and tools your team uses.

02

Design

We agree the greeting, questions to ask, details to capture and the situations that should be passed to a person.

03

Configure

We prepare the approved business information and call rules, then check the phone routing and any requested software connections for compatibility.

04

Rigorous Test

We stress-test the agent with representative caller paths, interruptions, ambiguous questions, edge cases and escalation routes before any customer speaks to it.

05

Deploy

We connect your live business phone number via conditional or full call-forwarding. We conduct live verification calls alongside your team before opening public call traffic.

06

Active Management

Our team continuously monitors system uptime, telephony health, and call delivery pipelines. We address issues proactively and implement your approved operational changes quickly.

07

Continuous Optimisation

We review representative caller transcripts each month, refine prompt phrasing to reduce misunderstandings, update pricing or seasonal details, and optimize conversion rates.
