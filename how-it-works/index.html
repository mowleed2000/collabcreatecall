Our 7-Stage Customer Lifecycle

How we take your call handling from friction to flow.

Every implementation follows a structured engineering methodology designed to protect your brand reputation and maximize qualified lead capture.

01

Discover

We analyze your historical call logs, peak traffic times, common customer FAQs, pricing logic, service boundaries, and current booking workflows to identify the exact friction points.

02

Design

We map the conversational tree: greeting tone, intent qualification paths, required data fields (name, phone, location, job details), and safety escalation triggers for emergencies.

03

Configure

We build the agent's knowledge base, set strict guardrails to prevent hallucinations, configure telephony numbers, and establish API connections into your calendar and CRM software.

04

Rigorous Test

We stress-test the agent with hundreds of simulated test calls: background noise, interruptions, ambiguous questions, edge cases, and emergency handoffs before any customer speaks to it.

05

Deploy

We connect your live business phone number via conditional or full call-forwarding. We conduct live verification calls alongside your team before opening public call traffic.

06

Active Management

Our team continuously monitors system uptime, telephony health, and call delivery pipelines. We address issues proactively and implement your approved operational changes quickly.

07

Continuous Optimisation

We review representative caller transcripts each month, refine prompt phrasing to reduce misunderstandings, update pricing or seasonal details, and optimize conversion rates.

Honest Scope

Transparent boundaries and platform compatibility.

We believe in commercial clarity. The capabilities of your system depend on your specific tier, telephony setup, and software APIs.

Telephony Setup

Works via standard call forwarding from BT, Vodafone, RingCentral, Gamma, mobile SIMs, and VoIP providers. You do not need to replace your hardware or phone provider.

Software Compatibility

Direct calendar synchronization requires standard cloud calendar access (Google Workspace, Office 365, or Calendly). Custom CRM endpoints depend on API availability.

Ongoing management includes agreed monthly updates for pricing, hours, and script tweaks. Major new workflows or extra branches can be scoped as needed.
