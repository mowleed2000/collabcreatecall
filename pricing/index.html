What the proposal covers

Understand what you’re paying for.

A proposal should describe the service and the costs that apply to your setup in plain terms.

01

Setup and call design

Discovery, agreed call flows, approved business information, phone routing and pre-launch checks included in the scope.

02

Ongoing support

Any updates, reviews or ongoing management are listed with their frequency and limits, so you know what is covered.

03

Phone and software costs

Requested integrations are checked for compatibility. Any separate telephony, AI usage or software charges should be identified before you proceed.

What affects the scope

Every call handling setup is different.

These are the details we use to understand what your business needs before outlining a suitable scope.

Call volume and coverage

Approximate call levels, busy periods and the hours when you want the agent to answer.

Call types and routing

The enquiries to handle, information to capture and situations that should go to your team.

Phone and software setup

Your current number, phone provider and any calendar, booking or CRM connection you want assessed.

Before you decide

Know what is included before work begins.

Ask for the proposal to set out the agreed call flow, phone routing, integrations, support, any usage limits and costs that apply. If a connection depends on another provider or system, its compatibility should be confirmed as part of scoping.

Need a price for your business?

Tell us what calls you receive and what you want the agent to do. We’ll use that context to discuss a relevant walkthrough and the next step.

Pricing questions

Useful details before a conversation.

Why isn’t there one fixed price on this page?

The scope can vary with call volume, routing, requested integrations and ongoing support. A quote should reflect the setup discussed with you.

Are phone and AI usage costs included?

That depends on the proposed setup. Ask for any third-party or usage charges to be clearly identified before you decide.

Can I keep my current business number?

Call forwarding may let you keep it, depending on your phone provider and current setup. We can review the available routing options with you.
