Private Healthcare

Physiotherapy, Dental, Osteopathy & Medical Clinics

Configure Healthcare Agent →

⚠️ The Operational Bottleneck

Clinic receptionists balance in-person patient arrivals, payment processing, prescription paperwork, and ringing phones. Callers seeking urgent advice may need a clear route to the appropriate member of the practice team.

⚡ Illustrative Call Workflow

  1. Answers with compassionate, calm, clinical tone of voice.
  2. Asks structured triage questions (pain level, duration, private insurance vs self-pay).

Professional Services

Accountants, Legal Practices & Mortgage Brokers

Sub-specialties: Chartered accountancy, conveyancing solicitors, commercial finance, wealth advisory.

  5. Can capture agreed caller details and pass them to your team using an available, compatible system.
