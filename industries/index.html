Trades & Home Services

Plumbing, Heating, Electrical, Roofing & HVAC

Sub-specialties: Emergency boiler repairs, commercial HVAC, roofing contractors, domestic drainage.

Configure Trades Voice Agent →

⚠️ The Operational Bottleneck

Engineers and contractors are on ladders, in roof voids, or under pipework with their hands full. When an emergency leak or heating outage strikes, homeowners call the next firm on Google if the phone rings more than 3 times. Over £1,800/week in high-margin emergency jobs are forfeited to voicemail.

⚡ Illustrative Call Workflow

  1. Answers instantly on ring 2 with your business name and friendly tone.
  2. Triages between critical emergencies (active leak, gas smell, power outage) and standard routine estimates.
  3. Captures exact postal code, property access instructions, and tenancy/owner status.
  4. Checks on-call engineer roster or offers guaranteed arrival time window.
  5. Dispatches SMS booking confirmation to caller and push alert to field engineer.

Automotive & Garages

Independent Garages, MOT Centres & Bodyshops

Sub-specialties: MOT testing stations, German specialist mechanics, tyre centres, accident repair.

Configure Automotive Agent →

⚠️ The Operational Bottleneck

Technicians are under vehicle ramps with greasy gloves. Receptionists are fielding 50+ identical calls daily: "Is my car ready for collection?", "Can I squeeze in an MOT this Thursday?", "How much for front brake pads on an Audi A4?" Phone queues lead to missed booking requests and frustrated motorists.

⚡ Illustrative Call Workflow

  1. Collects registration number (e.g., "LD21 XYZ") and validates vehicle make/model instantly.
  2. Answers collection status queries directly from your garage management system.
  3. Schedules MOT slots and service ramps based on real-time technician bay capacity.
  4. Logs symptom details (e.g. grinding noise during braking, engine warning light) for technician pre-read.
  5. Sends SMS drop-off reminder with morning arrival instructions and keys drop-box code.

Private Healthcare

Physiotherapy, Dental, Osteopathy & Medical Clinics

Sub-specialties: Private dentistry, sports physio, chiropractic, dermatology, outpatient clinics.

Configure Healthcare Agent →

⚠️ The Operational Bottleneck

Clinic receptionists balance in-person patient arrivals, payment processing, prescription paperwork, and ringing phones. Callers seeking urgent relief or initial evaluations abandon calls if put on hold for more than 45 seconds, booking with alternative local clinics.
