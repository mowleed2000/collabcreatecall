Trades & Home Services

Plumbing, Heating, Electrical, Roofing & HVAC

Sub-specialties: Emergency boiler repairs, commercial HVAC, roofing contractors, domestic drainage.

Configure Trades Voice Agent →

⚠️ The Operational Bottleneck

Engineers and contractors are on ladders, in roof voids, or under pipework with their hands full. When an emergency leak or heating outage strikes, callers may try another provider if they cannot reach the business. Missed calls can delay urgent enquiries and leave teams without the details they need to follow up.

⚡ Illustrative Call Workflow

  1. Greets callers using your business name and agreed call flow.
  2. Triages between critical emergencies (active leak, gas smell, power outage) and standard routine estimates.
  3. Captures exact postal code, property access instructions, and tenancy/owner status.
  4. Captures the caller’s location and request, then follows the escalation process agreed with your team.
  5. Dispatches SMS booking confirmation to caller and push alert to field engineer.

Salons & Aesthetics

Hair Salons, Barbershops & Aesthetic Clinics

Sub-specialties: Premium barbers, medical aesthetics, balayage studios, skin treatment centers.

⚠️ The Operational Bottleneck

Front-of-house staff are performing precision treatments, foil applications, or skin peels. Stopping mid-treatment to answer the phone disrupts the client experience. After 6:00 PM, customers trying to book evening or weekend appointments get voicemail and switch to a competitor.

Private Healthcare

Physiotherapy, Dental, Osteopathy & Medical Clinics

Sub-specialties: Private dentistry, sports physio, chiropractic, dermatology, outpatient clinics.

Configure Healthcare Agent →

⚠️ The Operational Bottleneck

Clinic receptionists balance in-person patient arrivals, payment processing, prescription paperwork, and ringing phones. Callers seeking urgent advice may need a clear route to the appropriate member of the practice team.

⚡ Illustrative Call Workflow

  1. Answers with compassionate, calm, clinical tone of voice.
  2. Asks structured triage questions (pain level, duration, private insurance vs self-pay).
  3. Screens for clinical red flags (e.g. chest pain, severe neurological signs) and directs immediately to 999/111 if safety thresholds are met.
  4. Books initial consultation with the appropriate specialist practitioner.
  5. Can capture agreed administrative details for your team. Clinical decisions and urgent care remain with qualified staff.

🔗 CRM & Software Integrations

Compatible with Cliniko, Pabau, WriteUpp, Dentally, Carestream, Semble with full GDPR data redaction.

Professional Services

Accountants, Legal Practices & Mortgage Brokers

Sub-specialties: Chartered accountancy, conveyancing solicitors, commercial finance, wealth advisory.

Configure Professional Agent →

⚠️ The Operational Bottleneck

Highly paid solicitors, partners, and brokers lose 10+ hours per week fielding unqualified calls from price-shoppers or clients seeking services outside the firm's core focus. Simultaneously, high-value corporate inquiries outside office hours call competitors who pick up first.

⚡ Illustrative Call Workflow

  4. Directly schedules a qualified 30-minute discovery call into the appropriate fee earner's diary.
