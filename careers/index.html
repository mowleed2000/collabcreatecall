The Opportunity

A compelling, tangible B2B sales product.

As a Sales Representative with Collab Create, you are not selling abstract consulting or commodity utilities. You are selling a concrete operational solution to business owners who are physically losing money every day because they cannot answer the phone while working.

You demonstrate our live voice engine — allowing prospective clients to call our sandbox number from their own mobile and hear how naturally it triages emergencies, quotes package prices, and books calendar slots.

You control your diary, choose your target sectors, and earn high upfront commissions on system setups plus monthly recurring commissions on active client retainers.
