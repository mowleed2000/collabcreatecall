The Opportunity

A compelling, tangible B2B sales product.

As a Sales Representative with Collab Create, you are not selling abstract consulting or commodity utilities. You introduce a call handling service to businesses and discuss whether it could suit their needs.

You discuss the prospect’s call handling needs and arrange a tailored walkthrough focused on the calls their team receives.

The role is self-employed. Any commission structure and payment terms are agreed before you begin.

Target Sectors

Where Voice AI solves immediate, painful bottlenecks.

You have full freedom to target any inbound telephone-reliant business across the UK:

🔧

Trades & Home Services

Emergency plumbers, heating engineers, roofers, electricians. Triage active leaks and boiler breakdowns during agreed coverage hours.

🚗

Automotive & Garages

Independent MOT centres, tyre shops, bodyshops. Answer repetitive quotes and collection updates.

✂️

Salons & Aesthetics

Barbershops, hair salons, skin clinics. Capture high-volume evening bookings without interrupting treatments.

🏥

Private Healthcare

Physiotherapy, dental, chiropractic clinics. Calm, empathetic triage and new patient intake.

🍽️

Hospitality & Venues

Restaurants, rooftop bars, private hire event spaces. Group dining and venue buyout lead qualification.

⚖️

Professional Services

Accountants, conveyancers, mortgage brokers. High-value inbound screening and discovery booking.
