Conversion-Oriented Call Sequence

Phase 1: Inbound & Intent

Caller dials in → Natural greeting → Identifies reason for calling within first 10 seconds.

Phase 2: Questioning & Triage

Asks targeted clarifying questions → Verifies customer contact details and job location.

Phase 3: Action Execution

Books appointment slot OR routes call OR logs callback request with verified priority level.

Phase 4: Post-Call Sync

Sends SMS confirmation to caller → Syncs transcript & payload to CRM → Team notified.

Safety & Escalation

Never trapped in an automated loop: Human handoff when it matters.

Callers must always be treated with respect. When a conversation requires human judgement, empathy, or specialist knowledge, the system escalates seamlessly.

1. Urgent Emergencies

High-stakes situations (e.g. burst pipes, gas smell, severe pain, vehicle stranded on motorway) trigger immediate live warm-transfer to on-call mobile lines.

2. Complex or Custom Enquiries

When a caller asks questions outside the approved knowledge base, the agent does not fabricate answers. It captures the exact requirement and schedules an agreed callback.

3. Caller Request for Human

If a caller explicitly asks to speak to a person, the system respects their preference immediately without arguing or repeating robotic scripts.

Outbound Scope & Compliance

Permitted outbound workflows: Respectful, useful, and compliant.

We do not offer or build aggressive, unsolicited cold-calling robocallers. UK direct marketing regulations (PECR, GDPR, TPS/CTPS) strictly govern automated calling.

Instead, we configure high-value, permissioned outbound workflows that support your existing customer base and warm enquiries:

Appointment Reminders: Automated call to confirm attendance for high-value clinic or trade visits, reducing costly no-shows.

Requested Callbacks: Responding to web form enquiries within 2 minutes while the buyer is still at their desk.

Job Completion Follow-ups: Verifying customer satisfaction and prompting for a Google review after a service visit.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions about our AI voice agent service.

How does the agent sound to a UK customer?

Our agents utilize high-fidelity UK voices calibrated for natural phrasing, realistic conversational cadence, and professional business tone. Callers regularly comment on the speed and clarity of the service compared to waiting on hold.

Do I have to change my advertised business telephone number?

No. You keep your current number exactly as it is. We issue a dedicated agent connection number. You then configure standard call forwarding on your line — either 24/7, overflow when busy, or out-of-hours only.

Can it replace my human receptionist or staff?

The system is designed to support and protect your human staff, not necessarily eliminate them. In most businesses, the agent handles the 80% of calls that are repetitive, routine, or after-hours, freeing your front-of-house team to deliver exceptional service to clients in the room.

How is caller data protected under UK GDPR?

All data processing, call recording, and transcription are conducted in compliance with UK GDPR and Data Protection regulations. Caller data is transmitted via encrypted channels to your approved systems, with appropriate data retention and privacy policies applied.

How much does the service cost?

We provide clear, transparent pricing based on your call volume and required software integrations. We structure packages with a one-time engineering setup fee and a predictable monthly management and telephony allowance. We provide detailed written proposals following your initial consultation.

What do you need from us to begin?

Following our initial consultation, we conduct an operational onboarding session where we collect your service descriptions, pricing guidance, frequently asked questions, operating hours, and software access details. We handle the rest.
