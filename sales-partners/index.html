Partner FAQ

Frequently asked questions about selling voice AI.

Do I need technical or coding knowledge?

No. You focus purely on the commercial conversation: understanding the business's call volume, explaining how missed calls cost them revenue, and demonstrating the agent. CCM's engineering team handles 100% of the conversation design, prompt engineering, CRM integrations, and ongoing maintenance.

What support and collateral do you provide?

You receive access to industry-specific case study data, live demonstration numbers your prospects can dial, cold calling and outreach scripts, objection handling battlecards, and direct Slack/WhatsApp access to our team.

How are commissions calculated and paid?

You receive a substantial upfront fee when the client's voice system is commissioned, plus an ongoing percentage of their monthly management retainer for as long as they remain a paying customer.
