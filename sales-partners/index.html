Partner FAQ

Frequently asked questions about selling voice AI.

Do I need technical or coding knowledge?

No. You focus purely on the commercial conversation: understanding the business's call volume, explaining how missed calls cost them revenue, and demonstrating the agent. CCM handles the setup and agreed ongoing support.

What support and collateral do you provide?

You receive access to industry-specific case study data, Request a Demonstration numbers your prospects can dial, cold calling and outreach scripts, objection handling battlecards, and direct Slack/WhatsApp access to our team.

How are commissions calculated and paid?

Commission structure, payment timing and any ongoing share are agreed with each partner in advance.
