How the Live Voice Stack Operates

A live voice call involves four synchronized processing engines operating under strict latency constraints:

1
Voice Activity Detection & Streaming STT

Deepgram Nova-2 / Whisper engines transcribe British phonemes in under 150ms.

2
Guardrailed Reasoning Engine (LLM)

Claude 3.5 Sonnet / GPT-4o parses intent, cross-references your knowledge base, and executes tools.

3
Ultra-Low-Latency TTS Synthesis

Cartesia Sonic / ElevenLabs Turbo generates natural British voice inflection in under 120ms.

4
Telephony & CRM Webhook Dispatch

Twilio SIP trunks, Retell, or Vapi WebRTC connect directly to your CRM API via webhook payloads.

Technical & Operational FAQ

Frequently asked questions about voice AI capabilities.

Can callers tell that it is an AI voice agent?

Our voice models operate with sub-600ms latency, natural British speech cadence, and context-aware pauses. Callers immediately notice how prompt and helpful the response is. We also recommend full transparency: having the agent introduce itself as your automated assistant creates trust while setting caller expectations for quick, accurate service.

What happens if a caller interrupts or talks over the agent?

Unlike legacy IVR ("Press 1 for Sales") systems, our voice engine features full-duplex Voice Activity Detection. If a caller says "Wait, actually it’s a different postcode," the agent stops speaking immediately, absorbs the correction, and adapts the response without restart or hesitation.

How does the system handle complex or unexpected questions?

We implement strict guardrails. The agent only speaks on confirmed business policies, services, and approved ranges. When faced with an out-of-scope query, it politely acknowledges the request, captures the details, and transfers the caller to an on-duty staff member or flags an urgent callback ticket.

Can the agent dial out for appointment reminders or lapsed customer recalls?

Yes. Outbound workflows are deployed strictly within agreed commercial parameters: service due reminders (e.g. annual boiler service or MOT due date), appointment confirmation calls, and follow-ups on web enquiry form submissions within 60 seconds.

How quickly can our company be up and running?

A typical managed implementation takes 7 to 10 business days from discovery to live deployment. This allows sufficient time for rigorous scenario stress testing, CRM webhook validation, and staff training before connecting live phone traffic.
